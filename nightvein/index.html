Building a little
world of green.

The grow room behind Night Vein Anthuriums, created by Sharon Mitchell in Kansas.

A framework for growing

Sharon’s grow room begins with a powder-coated green tubular frame. The semi-modular structure gives the room its framework, with VIVOSUN reflective Mylar-coated walls enclosing the growing space. It is a practical setting for a collection whose details deserve a closer look.

Light, mist, and warmth

Grow lights illuminate the foliage, while the reflective walls help distribute that light around the enclosure. Misters add moisture to the air, and heat lamps supply warmth. Together, these elements shape the warm, humid environment Sharon has created for her plants; the lamps provide heat, while misting supplies the moisture.

The plants at the heart of it

Anthuriums are the focus of Night Vein: heart-shaped leaves, rich velvet surfaces, striking veins, and new growth that can emerge in a completely different color from the mature foliage. Her Instagram journal includes Golden Child, Delta Force, Red Vein Dark Phoenix, and a range of papillilaminum and magnificum crosses. Alongside the anthuriums, Sharon also grows monsteras.

Designed with a personal touch

As a full-time architectural drafter, Sharon works with spaces and structures every day. Her grow room brings that same interest in making a space her own into a deeply personal project. Night Vein is a place where her creative life and love of plants meet.
